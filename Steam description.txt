Rise of Nations is a mod with the objective of creating an unforgettable century of gameplay (1900-2030). Currently having development up until 1949 we aim to be a mod about long runs and letting the player choose the way they want to shape the world such as perhaps starting in 1900 in the Dawn of a New Century, the 1910's with the Balkan Powder Keg, 1919 when the smoke settles, 1933 with the Rise of Evil, 1936 with the start of the world in turmoil, 1939 the point of no Return, and 1949 with the start of a new war between two spheres of Influence!

It continues the legacy of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1829382334]Totaler Krieg 2030[/url]

[h1]We would be pleased if you join our team and help Rise of Nations out. Jut note that you need modding experience to join it. If you don't have it, making bug reports will help. Join our Discord here: https://discord.gg/wW3nmgP4Vd[/h1]

[h2]PLEASE NOTE THAT AT THE MOMENT, THE DATES AFTER 1949, ARE NOT FULLY FUNCTIONAL OR EMPTY, PLAY AT YOUR OWN RISK[/h2]
[h2]THIS MOD IS ENGLISH ONLY AND IT WILL BE IN ENGLISH ONLY FOR ETERNITY[/h2]
[h2]MOD IS COMPATIBLE WITH 1.19.2[/h2]
